Independent Projects

Ugamu

TV Series Project | Pre-production

When Ugamu began to be Oscar’s imaginary friend, he never thought that he would spend so much time with him. Now Oscar is 15 years old and Ugamu is desperate.

Credits: Original Concept, Direction & Animation by Fabián Guamaní Aldaz
Distinctions: Winning Project at MIFA Pitching Sessions (Annecy 2018), Ciclic Animation Residence (France 2019), and Best Animated Series Project (Andimation, Denmark 2016).

Frontera / Border

Animated Short Film | 2D Digital | 2017

A beautifully crafted animated short film exploring borders, boundaries, and human connections through unique visual storytelling.

Credits: Direction and Animation by Fabián Guamaní Aldaz.
Distinctions: Official Selection at Annecy International Film Festival (2017), Animation Festival Anima Latina (2017), International Animated Films Festival KROK (2017), Lisbon Animated Film Festival MONSTRA (2018), and Indie-AniFest (2019).

Gran Lago / Big Lake

Short Film | 2D Digital | In Production

Many generations of fishermen have lived around a lake keeping a vital connection with it. A drought dries up the lake and fishermen set on a journey to faraway lands. Only one decides to stay. He knows the lake’s destiny is his destiny too.

Credits: Script, Direction, Concept Art & Animation by Fabián Guamaní Aldaz
Distinctions: MIFA Pitching Sessions - Animation du Monde, Annecy International Film Festival, 2020.
