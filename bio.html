Biography

I’m a Creative Director, 2D Animator, and Motion Graphic Designer specializing in cinematic storytelling.

Born in Quito and currently residing in Tbilisi, my background is shaped by high-caliber international experiences, including being selected for the Andimation lab organized by The Animation Workshop in Denmark (2016), winning the Andean Call award (2017), completing a residency at Ciclic in France (2019), and participating in collective projects in South Korea.

I balance my dedication to cinematic film projects with my freelance work in motion design and explainer videos. If you are looking for a collaborator who can bring cinematic depth to commercial motion or join forces on a film project, I'd love to connect!
