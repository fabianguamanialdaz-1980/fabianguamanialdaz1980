Biography

Animated IP Creator, Director, 2D Animator, and Motion Graphic Designer specializing in cinematic storytelling.

Born in Quito and currently residing in Tbilisi, my background is shaped by high-caliber international experiences, including being selected for the animation project lab organized by The Animation Workshop in Denmark (2016), winning the Andean Call award (2017), winning the MIFA Pitching Session at the Annecy International Animation Film Festival (2018), and completing a residency at Ciclic in France (2019).

I balance my dedication to cinematic film projects with my freelance work in motion design and explainer videos. If you are looking for a collaborator who can bring cinematic depth to commercial motion or join forces on a film project, I'd love to connect!
